Background

Economics at the core.
Evidence throughout.

I am an economics researcher based in Pakistan, with a BS in Economics from the University of Sargodha and an MPhil in Economics from Bahauddin Zakariya University. Since 2022, I have worked remotely as a Research Associate with ZAR Management Consultancies in Dubai, contributing to economic and financial research, quantitative data analysis, research reports and presentations. My interests include inclusive growth, economic development, financial research, business analytics and research methodology. I value accurate sources, transparent methods, careful interpretation, reproducibility where practical and clear communication.

I have also undertaken independent and academic work involving econometrics, statistical modelling, survey analysis, financial analysis and critical assessment of technical research. The selected samples are separate from employment and describe their evidence and contribution limits explicitly.
