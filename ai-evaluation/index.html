AI evaluation & research review

Research quality assurance for analytical reports, statistical interpretation and technical documentation.

Supporting service

Evidence and methodology review

I offer research quality review for analytical documents, including AI-assisted economic reports, technical summaries, slide decks and charts.

The review can examine whether sources are traceable, numbers are consistent, methods are appropriate and conclusions are supported by the available evidence.

This is both an available service and an area of professional interest. It does not imply AI-lab employment, completed AI-training contracts or formal journal peer-review appointments.
