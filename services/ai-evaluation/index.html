Review AI-assisted analytical content against its evidence.

Who this is for

Research teams, content evaluators and organisations using AI-assisted reports.

What I can do

  • Evidence-to-claim auditing and numerical consistency checks

  • Reference checks and review of economic reports, decks and charts
