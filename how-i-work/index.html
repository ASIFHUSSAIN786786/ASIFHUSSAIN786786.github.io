07 / Questions, answered

Before we
begin.

A few practical details about research support, deliverables and working together.

Can you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I am based in Pakistan and available for remote collaboration with international clients. Communication arrangements and timelines are agreed before work begins.

What services can I request?

Economic and policy research, data preparation, statistical and econometric analysis, business and market analysis, and reports, dashboards and data visualisation. The scope depends on your question and available evidence.

How does the free trial work?

We agree on one small task, one deliverable and a delivery date before work starts. The trial is limited to three hours and one task per prospective client, followed by a short feedback discussion.

Is the trial completely free?

Yes. There is no payment or subscription obligation. The scope must be agreed in advance and use public or appropriately authorised data.

What happens after the trial?

We discuss your feedback. If you would like to continue, I prepare a paid proposal with agreed deliverables, fees, timing and revision terms. There is no obligation to proceed.

Which statistical and analytical tools do you use?

Stata and Microsoft Excel are my primary professional tools. I also have analytical experience or training in Power BI, SQL, R, Python and SPSS. Tools and specialist requirements are confirmed for each scope; training credentials are not presented as employment experience.

Can you analyse an existing dataset?

Yes, subject to a review of its structure, quality, variable definitions and your permission to use it. Suitable methods and limitations are discussed before analysis.

Can you help interpret statistical outputs?

Yes. I can explain descriptive summaries, statistical tests and model outputs in clear language. When the original dataset or scripts are unavailable, that limitation is explicitly disclosed.

Will I receive editable datasets, documents, dashboards, or code?

Editable outputs can be included when agreed. File formats, scripts, dashboard delivery and any software requirements are specified in the project scope.

How do you determine pricing and deadlines?

Pricing and timing depend on the objective, data condition, analytical complexity and deliverables. You receive an agreed scope and quote before paid work begins.

Can you handle confidential information?

Confidentiality and secure sharing arrangements can be agreed before work begins. Please do not send confidential or sensitive datasets through the initial enquiry form.

Can you support academic research?

Yes, through legitimate research assistance, transparent methodology and ethical research practices. This does not include fabricated data, invented citations, guaranteed academic results or undisclosed ghostwriting.

How is artificial intelligence used in your workflow?

AI tools may be used for suitable drafting or language-editing tasks where permitted. Any restrictions can be agreed before work begins. Research sources, findings, and calculations require human review.
