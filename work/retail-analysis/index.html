Project brief

Question

Turn reported retail summaries into a readable business analysis.

Analytical scope

Revision of supplied summary outputs for 51,290 records, 2011–2014.

Presented output

KPI summary, mean-versus-median comparison and interpretation of distribution and profit limits.

Practical interpretation

Averages and positive aggregate profit do not describe every record; segment-level work requires the original workbook.

A readable business summary

This revised business analysis sample presents the supplied retail summary outputs in a concise overview. It examines aggregate sales and profit, the profit-to-sales ratio, and the difference between mean and median sales per record.

Source and analytical scope

Reported values come from supplied analytical outputs. The supplied Retail Analysis Sample PDF identifies a descriptive-statistics screenshot in Buisness report(1).docx, also present in Buisness report.docx, as its source. The reported period is 2011–2014. The original workbook, data provenance, currency and accounting definitions were not confirmed. Methods presented here are descriptive summary interpretation and visual comparison; no new record-level analysis is claimed.

The mean is higher than the median, which is consistent with larger sales records pulling the average upward. The reported sales skewness is 8.17, and the mean is about 2.90 times the median. These summaries indicate uneven sales values, while the full distribution, frequency of high-value records and influence of individual outliers still require record-level data.

The aggregate profit-to-sales ratio is approximately 11.62%, based on total reported profit divided by total reported sales. It is an aggregate ratio, not the mean of record-level margins. The mean discount is reported as 14.29%; it is an unweighted record-level mean according to the supplied PDF. An average discount does not establish additional sales or improved profit.

Limits of summary-only interpretation

Currency, accounting definitions and the meaning of a record are unconfirmed. Reported profit ranges from −6,599.98 to 8,399.98 per record, so positive aggregate profit coexists with individual loss-making records. No regional, category, customer or monthly breakdowns are available. These outputs do not establish that discounts caused changes in sales or profit. Independent validation is limited without the original workbook and scripts. Product, customer, regional and monthly summaries, validation of record identifiers and monetary units, and a review of loss-making records are proposed next steps—not completed findings.

Contribution and available samples

The verified classification is a revised business analysis sample based on supplied outputs. This presentation communicates those summary results without claiming independent replication. The sample transcribes and rounds reported figures and uses AI-assisted language and presentation; original workbook totals were not independently replicated.
